\noindent\textbf{Manuscript Title:} On the Non-Negativity of Longitudinal Velocity Autocorrelations in Homogeneous Isotropic Turbulence: Kinematic Constraints, Convexity, and Spectral Broadening

\vspace{1.5em}

\noindent\textbf{Statement of Significance:}

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent
This paper provides a theoretical and numerical foundation for whether longitudinal velocity autocorrelations $f(r)$ in homogeneous isotropic turbulence are universally non-negative. From first principles, we show that positive definiteness of the correlation tensor fails to enforce $f(r) \ge 0$ via Schoenberg's theorem, while 1D spectral convexity connects to $f(r) \ge 0$ through P\'olya's criterion. We construct a smooth, divergence-free, finite-energy counterexample exhibiting a genuine negative loop. Furthermore, we reveal spectral bandwidth as the governing parameter, with nonlinear triad interactions rapidly broadening narrowband states over an eddy-turnover time, suppressing negative loops and establishing the robust non-negativity observed in mature turbulence.
